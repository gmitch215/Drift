SolveCertificate

object SolveCertificate(source)

Turns a solve into a case directory: the 6b2 files, the preregistered specs, the arms, a hash-chained results/ that continues the observation chain, and certificate.json. 6d4 packs these files into a .driftcase and re-derives the posteriors and statistics from them.
