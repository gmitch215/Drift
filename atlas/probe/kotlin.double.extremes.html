Notes

Three groups, three mechanisms. JDK digits (4.9E-324, E308) and ECMAScript digits (5e-324, e+308) are each what their platform page describes, so min-value, max-value and min-normal split by platform; js-ir also prints neg-zero and underflow as 0 and 2^53+1 as 9007199254740992. The wasm family prints the ECMAScript digits with a Kotlin layout (9007199254740992.0, 0.0) that no spec explains: KT-88414 Fixed, equal to the JVM on the 2.5.0-Beta1 wasm transcript. KT-88036 (Open) says the K/JS Double.MIN_VALUE.toString rounds, where the JDK form would not; js-ir is unchanged on 2.5.0-Beta1 (measured with the standalone compiler). No Kotlin page specifies the Double.toString format. cloudflare-wasm prints what wasm prints; cloudflare-jvm prints what android, ios, jvm, jvm-17 and 5 more print.
