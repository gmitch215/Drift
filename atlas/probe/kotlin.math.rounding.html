Notes

Downgraded from documented: the KDoc says "nearest integer" and the inputs are not ties, so the Native and Wasm results (1, 4503599627370498, 8388610) violate it (KT-89074 Open, unchanged on 2.5.0-Beta1). cloudflare-jvm differs on the round lines and the roundToInt and roundToLong lines (0.5, 2.5, -0.5, -1.5, -2.5, 0.49999999999999994, 4503599627370497, float-neg-2.5): TeaVM 0.15.0 (the version bytebox 1.0.3 pins) implements rint as round and round as the signum formula above, which rounds ties away from zero, and adding 0.5 in float or double precision moves the large integer inputs. teavm#1263 describes the round formula and was closed on 2026-10-04, after that release, so its fix is not in the measured build. js-ir differs only in text (round=0 against 0.0). cloudflare-wasm prints what wasm prints.
